2025 · SUPERSPORT

Kawasaki
Ninja ZX-6R

Precision for the fast line.

THE QUICK READ

Track-bred focus.
Everyday possibility.

The ZX-6R brings a distinctive 636 cc inline-four to the middleweight supersport class. Its eager engine, sharp chassis and rider-focused electronics make it equally at home carving a favorite stretch of road or turning laps at a circuit.

$12,399 approx. US starting MSRP

At a glance

Engine
636 cc liquid-cooled inline-four
Power
About 124 hp (127 hp with ram air)
Transmission
6-speed, assist and slipper clutch
Top speed
About 257 km/h (160 mph)
Brakes
Dual front discs with ABS
Rider aids
Selectable power modes, traction control, quick shifter
01

Engine & performance

The oversquare 636 cc engine delivers lively midrange response as well as the high-revving character riders expect from a supersport. A close-ratio six-speed gearbox and optional quick shifter help keep acceleration flowing on track.

02

Design & handling

Race-inspired bodywork, a compact riding position and a stiff aluminum perimeter frame give the Ninja its purposeful stance. The adjustable suspension and powerful dual front brakes are designed for confident, precise cornering.

03

Why riders choose it

Its slightly larger displacement than many middleweight rivals adds useful flexibility without losing the engaging feel of a four-cylinder sportbike. Multiple power and traction-control settings let riders tailor the response to the conditions.

GOOD TO KNOW

A committed supersport riding position favors control and feedback. Take time to find a comfortable fit, and save high-speed riding for a closed course.

Big character. Open horizons.

01

Engine & performance

The 890 cc CP3 triple is known for its distinctive crossplane-inspired pulse and flexible delivery. A broad spread of torque makes it responsive in everyday riding, while the quick shifter supports smooth upshifts and downshifts.

02

Design & handling

Minimal bodywork, a compact LED headlight and an upright riding position give the MT-09 its muscular, stripped-back look. Its lightweight chassis and adjustable suspension encourage quick direction changes and relaxed city maneuvering.

03

Why riders choose it

The MT-09 balances strong performance with an approachable, upright posture. Its selectable electronic settings, color display and everyday-friendly ergonomics make it a versatile choice for spirited rides and regular commutes.

GOOD TO KNOW

The upright layout offers comfort and visibility, but the bike's torque is substantial. Choose a gentle ride mode while getting familiar with the throttle.

2025 · SUPERBIKE

BMW
S 1000 RR

Engineered to own the apex.

THE QUICK READ

Precision at
superbike pace.

The S 1000 RR is BMW Motorrad's high-performance liter-class sportbike. A powerful 999 cc inline-four, advanced electronics and aerodynamic winglets combine to deliver a focused machine for experienced riders and closed-course performance.

$18,295 approx. US starting MSRP

At a glance

Engine
999 cc liquid-cooled inline-four
Power
About 205 hp
Torque
About 113 Nm
Top speed
About 303 km/h (188 mph), unrestricted
Transmission
6-speed with shift assistant
Rider aids
Riding modes, ABS Pro, traction and wheelie control
01

Engine & performance

The high-revving four-cylinder uses BMW's ShiftCam variable intake camshaft technology to support a broad powerband. A bidirectional shift assistant and configurable riding modes help manage its substantial performance.

02

Aerodynamics & control

Purpose-shaped fairings and winglets help generate downforce at speed. An aluminum frame, adjustable suspension and cornering-sensitive electronic systems are designed to help experienced riders make precise inputs on a circuit.

03

Why riders choose it

The S 1000 RR brings race-focused engineering and a comprehensive suite of electronic aids to the road-legal superbike category. Its power modes and adjustable systems allow careful setup for different skill levels and environments.

GOOD TO KNOW

This is an extremely powerful motorcycle intended for experienced riders. Its top speed is not a public-road target; always ride within the law and your abilities.
